IoT smart walker that predicts falls

Many older adults are never shown how to use a walker or walking stick properly, so a small loss of balance turns into a fall. This walker watches how it is being used and warns before balance is lost.

  • Arduino Uno with dual MPU6050 IMUs, dual HX711 load cells and dual HC-SR04 ultrasonics, running fully standalone with no cloud.
  • Designed a sensor-fusion Fall Risk Prediction engine and a Dynamic Stability Reserve metric, plus near-fall detection and a Gait Symmetry Index for rehab tracking.
  • Estimated hardware cost under USD 30.

Autonomous rover for disaster-response reconnaissance

A ground rover that sweeps a disaster area, builds a live map, finds people and hazards, and guides survivors out by voice.

  • LIDAR SLAM (BreezySLAM) on a Raspberry Pi 5 builds a real-time map overlaid with a hazard risk heatmap.
  • Modified YOLOv8n with a risk-score head and a loss reweighted toward people, alongside a camera YOLOv9t model and an MQ-2 gas sensor for fire, smoke and gas.
  • Modified A* finds the safest, shortest evacuation path; reinforcement learning adapts the sweep around new hazards; a Flask dashboard streams telemetry for human oversight.

Dental malocclusion detection from X-rays

A YOLOv9 pipeline that finds malocclusion and analyses teeth alignment in dental X-rays, built to make orthodontic screening faster and easier to explain.

Smart Grocer: produce spoilage detection and inventory

A multimodal system that spots produce going bad on the rack, item by item, so stores can act before it becomes waste.

  • YOLO pipeline classifies each item as fresh, ripening, at-risk or spoiled, with a rack-level anomaly baseline (rolling statistics, z-score).
  • Sensor-fusion freshness engine combines vision, gas/VOC and environment scores, and aims gas sampling at camera-detected items.
  • Planned barcode/OCR expiry tracking and a Firebase + Flutter app for live rack monitoring and waste analytics.

TriageIQ: real-time support ticket triage

Support tickets stream in, and each one is classified, prioritised, routed and given a drafted reply within seconds.

  • Tickets flow through Kafka to a classifier running a local LLM (qwen2.5 on Ollama), so nothing leaves the machine.
  • RAG over past resolved tickets with Qdrant and nomic embeddings drafts a reply for an agent to approve.
  • Live FastAPI dashboard streams the whole pipeline with server-sent events.

IoT-Enabled Smart Walker with Sensor-Fusion Fall Risk Prediction and Adaptive Mobility Assistance

Co-inventor, with Ponn Oviyaa S. Faculty guide: Dr. Subitha D. Inventive disclosure filed with IIPRD (Khurana & Khurana).

Many elderly people never learn to use their walking aid properly, and that is when falls happen. This walker reads tilt, weight balance and nearby obstacles together, and flags failing stability before a fall.

  • Bilateral fall detection and real-time weight-asymmetry monitoring.
  • Fall Risk Prediction engine, Dynamic Stability Reserve, near-fall detection and Gait Symmetry Index.
  • Standalone on an Arduino Uno, under USD 30 in hardware.
